# Hero Storyboard

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Deep engineering is made legible through depth, not decoration. The
page refuses the generic agency grid and the pseudo-terminal developer landing.

**OWN-WORLD:** Black water, the original engraving sampled into real ASCII,
sparse paper-white type, and green/ember illumination that exists inside the
image rather than the UI.

**STORY:** A visitor arrives with one difficult product problem, recognizes a
deep Java-first engineering network, sees evidence of real work, and emails
Ozero Dev.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A near-black macro water field fills the screen. Optical
energy stays in the lower-right while the left remains calm for a two-line offer
and one primary action. The wordmark and language control remain quiet at the
top edge. A literal fisherman is not required in this viewport.

**FORM:** A full-viewport cinematic field followed by a restrained editorial
proof sequence. The selected composition is “Quiet Water / Submerged Signal”;
it gives English and Russian copy stable negative space while preserving a
distinct material identity.

## Scroll sequence

1. **Stillness (0–35%)** — subtle black-water movement, readable offer and
   email CTA.
2. **Signal (35–65%)** — reflected lake/ember light passes beneath the surface;
   the original fisherman engraving appears as a restrained independent layer.
3. **Resolution (65–100%)** — the browser samples the engraving into a real
   code-native ASCII canvas while the copy resolves to “a lake of developers”.
4. **Release** — the stage yields to a compact proof line and normal document
   scrolling. No scroll-jacking continues beyond the hero.

## Media requirements

- No baked-in words, logos, UI, coordinates, or terminal glyphs.
- The WebP is the poster/fallback, not the final motion experience.
- The main hero uses a seamless code-native water treatment or a future
  atmospheric video loop; camera remains effectively locked.
- Desktop master: 16:9 with optical energy in the right/lower-right 45%.
- 4:3 crop: the water texture remains balanced without a required subject.
- Vertical: quiet upper field with optical energy below the copy.
- Static poster must carry the same contrast and focal hierarchy.
- Audio is unnecessary.
- ASCII characters are rendered from sampled `public/fishman.png` luminance;
  a generated contour-line image is not an acceptable substitute.
