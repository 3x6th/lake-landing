# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Ozero Dev serves founders, product leaders, and engineering leaders at startups
and growing companies in Europe and the CIS. They need to add delivery capacity
without spending months assembling an internal team.

## Product Purpose

The site must help a qualified visitor understand Ozero Dev's engagement model,
see concrete evidence of engineering capability, and start a conversation by
email. It is also the production artifact behind a public AI-assisted
development case study, but the marketing experience serves buyers first.

## Positioning

Ozero Dev is a Java-first talent network that can scale an engagement from one
engineer to a focused product squad in one to three weeks. Team extension is
the primary offer; managed product development and AI/knowledge integrations
are secondary offers.

## Operating Context

- Engagements may begin with one engineer or a small cross-functional squad.
- The minimum engagement is one month.
- The client normally owns roadmap and priorities for team-extension work.
- Ozero Dev can provide onboarding, technical oversight, mentoring,
  architecture support, and replacement support.
- An English-speaking subset of the network is available for international
  work; language fit is confirmed during matching.
- English is the primary site language; Russian is available at parity.
- The production site is statically hosted on GitHub Pages at `ozero.dev`.
- The primary conversion is an email conversation through a reachable address.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The curated network contains approximately 30 Java-focused engineers.
- A focused engagement can realistically start in one to three weeks.
- The two principals each have more than six years of production experience.
- Engineers in the network average approximately three years of experience.
- The network includes Java engineers, full-stack engineers, and project
  management capability; it does not claim a dedicated frontend department.
- Ozero Dev works with buyers in Europe and the CIS.
- The site must not publish pricing, founders' identities, team photographs,
  personal GitHub profiles, invented customers, testimonials, logos, outcomes,
  or completed-project claims.
- There is no dedicated team section or technology-logo wall.
- Technology is shown only when it explains real work.
- The site does not collect lead data; the first release uses `mailto:`.
- A domain mailbox is not confirmed yet, so the reachable configured address
  remains authoritative until it is replaced.

## Brand Commitments

- The name is Ozero Dev and the brand line is “a lake of developers”.
- Water is the central physical metaphor.
- The existing fisherman engraving was generated with Sora and may be
  regenerated or replaced while preserving the solitary boat-on-water motif.
- The committed direction is “Cinematic Engineering” with a smaller “Poetic
  Technology” influence: dark water, restrained iridescence, monochrome
  editorial typography, and one purposeful engraving-to-ASCII transition.
- XData is a reference for cinematic water and spatial pacing, not for its
  coordinate-heavy technical decoration.
- The Refero-derived Monopo Saigon reference informs typography, restraint,
  atmospheric media, and patient motion; it is not a design to clone.

## Evidence on Hand

- `public/fishman.png`: current Sora-generated fisherman engraving.
- Taska: a Jira/Kanban-like product in active development. Real interface
  screenshots may be published, but it must not be described as complete.
- AI knowledge assistant: an internal R&D product. An anonymized interface and
  architecture diagram may be published.
- Learning platform: a real but paused initiative. It should appear only if its
  paused status is explicit and it materially strengthens the story.
- No public customer logos, testimonials, or verified business-result metrics
  are available.

## Product Principles

1. Make the offer legible before making the experience cinematic.
2. Demonstrate engineering capability with real work, not generic claims.
3. Use one memorable visual mechanism instead of continuous spectacle.
4. State dynamic capacity conservatively and keep every commercial claim true.
5. Degrade media gracefully without degrading perceived craft.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must remain readable and operable with keyboard navigation, screen
readers, zoom, reduced motion, and constrained mobile hardware. Every critical
message and action must remain available when video or advanced motion is
disabled.
